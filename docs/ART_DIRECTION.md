# ART DIRECTION: THE HAUNTED CLOUD CITADEL
## AWS Virtual Hackathon 2026

### 1. High Concept & Spatial Metaphor
The experience is staged around a towering Victorian mansion standing in a foggy graveyard at midnight. A giant luminous moon anchors the distant horizon. Twisted gnarled trees, crooked tombstones, an ancient wrought-iron gate, hovering specters, glowing hand-carved jack-o'-lanterns, and supernatural cloud formations define the environment.

Warm pumpkin-orange illumination emanates from selected mansion windows, while vivid acid-green energy threads through architectural cloud-computing infrastructure. Scrolling does not merely slide pages—it travels visitors through distinct spatial zones of the estate, using the mansion and its chambers as an environmental metaphor for systems architecture, collaborative engineering, and problem solving.

### 2. Core Visual Qualities
- **Controlled Lighting & Deep Layering**: Sharp separation between foreground framing silhouettes (branches, gate spikes, fence posts), midground narrative subjects (mansion facade, server racks, challenge pedestals), and distant celestial elements (luminous moon, starfield, layered mist).
- **Art-Directed Stylization**: Premium stylized 3D avoiding juvenile cartoonishness and generic stock assets. Monochromatic black, charcoal, and stone surfaces punctuated by deliberate, vibrant emissive accents.
- **Atmospheric Depth**: Multi-layered volumetric ground fog planes with slow drift, rising orange embers, and sparse acid-green fireflies.
- **Negative Space for Typographic Dignity**: Intentional dark sky and mist clearings designed to guarantee high-contrast readability for oversized editorial headlines and critical date readouts.
- **Cohesive Hardware & Supernatural Synthesis**: Server rack blades, neural graph clusters, event-driven pulse conduits, and pipeline rails are woven into gothic stone architecture, illustrating modern cloud engineering inside a midnight world.

### 3. Palette Tokens
- **Obsidian (#050505)**: Dominant baseline void and canvas background.
- **Midnight (#080D1A)**: Atmospheric sky, distant horizons, and deep mist.
- **Charcoal (#171717)**: Stone foundations, mansion walls, and dark glass panels.
- **Moonlight (#C8CDD8)**: Cold celestial directional illumination and secondary body text.
- **Pumpkin (#FF7518)**: Primary interactive focus, window warmth, carved faces, and buttons.
- **Amber (#FFB000)**: DevOps pipelines, auxiliary indicators, and status highlights.
- **Acid Green (#A3FF12)**: Serverless pulses, cloud data conduits, and supernatural accents.
- **Electric Purple (#8B5CF6)**: Generative AI nodes and academic host highlights (flat color only, never gradient fills).

### 4. Explicit Exclusion List (Strict Defect Criteria)
The following elements are strictly forbidden across all copy, code, shaders, geometry, textures, and assets:
- **No Egyptian, Arabian, desert, dune, pyramid, or royal motifs, palettes, or iconography.**
- **No portfolio-specific, personal agency, or unrelated third-party references.**
- **No purple gradients or multi-color neon gradient fills on UI buttons, text, or borders.**
- **No pill-shaped buttons or rounded capsules.** Button radius is fixed at 6px, badge radius at 4px, card radius at 8px.
- **No AI-slop buzzwords**: unleash, empower, revolutionize, cutting-edge, next-generation, seamless, elevate, journey, game-changer, supercharge, "unlock the power".
- **No exclamation marks.**
- **No em dashes (—) or en dashes (–).** Ranges must be written with the word "to" (e.g., "October 31 to November 5, 2026").
- **No emoji anywhere** in copy, comments, UI, or metadata.
- **No fake statistics, invented participant counts, fake reviews, or simulated sponsors.**
- **No unauthorized corporate logos or third-party institutions.** Only the three recognized student communities exist: AWS Student Builder Groups, IIT Jodhpur, and GSMCOE.
